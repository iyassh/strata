## The day in one paragraph (for the paper's story)

three systems, five discoveries, and a verification protocol — because every
phase transition was preceded by a hostile gap hunt that the user insisted on.

family, a vacuous ground truth, a rotated file, an inflated headline), all

important scientific event: our own pre-registered falsifier fired against our
own best result, and the claim that survived — discovered models locate the
load-bearing invariants of healthy operation without being told where to look —
is stronger than the claim it replaced.
